# Third-party artwork in tile collections

The Animals, Fruits, Vegetables and Mixed symbols in `assets/collections/` come from the
"ZenMahjongJourney_PremiumCollections" pack. Its README says the pictograms were rendered from
**Noto Color Emoji** (Google, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji), and that the source
artwork is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 / Apache License 2.0 "as applicable".

**Action needed before a commercial release:** confirm which license applies to these rendered
images, and add the required attribution and license text to the game's credits/about screen
and the store listing. No credits screen exists yet.

The Alphabet letters, the ivory tile body and the overlays were rendered separately by the pack
author (per its README), not from Noto Emoji. Confirm their terms with the pack author too.
