Zen Mahjong Journey: Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Developer: Salman Abdallah · Contact: salman.abdallah.89@gmail.com

Zen Mahjong Journey ("the game") is a tile-matching puzzle game for Android and the web. This policy explains what information is used when you play and why. The developer does not run any servers for the game and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared levels, stars, best times and scores) and your settings (sound volumes, vibration, reduced animations, chosen tile collection, tutorial status) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer. On Android, the system may include them in your device's own backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on; you can turn this off in your Android settings. Uninstalling the game deletes this data from the device.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the level map, a full-screen ad between some levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a hint, a shuffle or a second chance. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, completed rewarded ads) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and regions with similar laws, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Settings → Privacy Options in the game. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.

The web version of the game shows no ads and uses no advertising services.

Google Play Games (Android app)

The Android app offers a Leaderboard through Google Play Games services. Signing in is optional; the game plays the same without it. When you sign in (or are signed in automatically because you use Play Games on your device), Google receives:

  • your Play Games player ID, gamer name and profile picture,
  • your total score (every level's best score added up), which is shown on the leaderboard to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.

This data is processed by Google, not by the developer, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile, its visibility and delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com. The web version has no sign-in and no leaderboard.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are loaded (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Progress and settings are kept only in your browser's own storage on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer and it will be handled promptly.

Security and retention

The developer holds no copy of your data. Data kept by Google for advertising and Play Games is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes

If the game adds features that use more data (for example cloud save or crash reporting), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the effective date above will change.
